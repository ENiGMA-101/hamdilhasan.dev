How I Plan to Review Apps and Software

I don't want my reviews to be just a list of features.

A feature can exist and still be frustrating to use.

So I plan to evaluate software from the perspective of actually using it.

What I will look at

1. First impression

Is it easy to understand what the application does?

2. Everyday usability

How does it feel after using it for several days?

3. Performance

Does it remain responsive with normal workloads?

4. Design

Is the interface clear, consistent and accessible?

5. Privacy and trust

What data does the product collect and what permissions does it request?

6. Value

Is the product worth the money or the time it asks from the user?

The most important rule

I will try to separate facts from my opinion.

That makes a review more useful and more honest.
