Building an Indoor Food Delivery Robot

One of my current project interests is an indoor food delivery robot.

The basic idea is simple: a small mobile robot should carry food from one point to another inside a controlled environment.

The engineering challenge is making the simple idea reliable.

What I am exploring

The project involves several areas:

  • motor control
  • obstacle detection
  • line detection
  • sensor integration
  • embedded programming
  • navigation logic
  • power management
  • physical design

Why this is interesting

A robot that moves from point A to point B sounds easy until the real world gets involved.

A sensor gives noisy readings. Motors don't behave identically. The robot can drift away from a line. An obstacle can appear unexpectedly.

Those problems make the project much more valuable as a learning experience.

Current direction

I'm experimenting with ESP32-based control and different sensor arrangements.

Future posts will document the circuit, control logic, testing results and the mistakes that happen along the way.

This page is intentionally a development log rather than a polished final report.
